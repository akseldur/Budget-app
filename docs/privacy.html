Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-08

This is a personal, non-commercial budget application built and used solely by its developer (a single user). It is not distributed to or used by any other individuals, and is not offered as a service to the public.

Data collected

Bank account and transaction data, retrieved via Enable Banking's Account Information Services (AIS) from the user's own bank accounts, with the user's own explicit consent through the bank's standard PSD2 authentication flow.

Use of data

Solely to categorize spending and track a personal monthly budget within the app. No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.

Sharing

Data is never shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party. It is stored in a private database controlled exclusively by the developer.
